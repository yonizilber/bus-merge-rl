# Explorer: bay or lane, under your conditions

The [results](RESULTS.md) fix some values to tell one story. The explorer fixes none: choose the traffic, the people per
car and on the bus, and the stop time in the lane and in the bay (separately, equal, or equal plus a penalty for the bay's
less precise stop), and it answers from the same {{stop:runs}} stored runs, with the 95% interval. Its starting values
are only a place to begin; the assumptions behind them, with their sources, are at the bottom of the tool and on the
[field-data](FIELD_DATA.md) page.
